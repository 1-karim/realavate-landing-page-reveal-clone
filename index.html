About

Hekaya is a growing archive of people, places and the small moments between them. The project is a collaboration between photographer Karim Ben Khelifa and writer/curator Rania Matar. The archive is intended to be a resource for artists, curators, and researchers interested in exploring the intersection of photography, storytelling, and social justice.
